What makes us human in the age of AI?

What should we protect as technology changes our lives?

May 2026

The question

As AI becomes more capable, what do we want it to do for us? In Magnifica Humanitas, Pope Leo XIV considers technology through Catholic social teaching, with human dignity and the common good at the centre.

The reading

Magnifica Humanitas

Pope Leo XIV · 15 May 2026

Read the full text at the Vatican →

Passages to read

Start with paragraphs 9, 109–110 and 112–113. They ask whose values technology carries, who has power over it and what a focus on efficiency can leave out.

“In practice, however, technology is never neutral, because it takes on the characteristics of those who devise, finance, regulate and use it.”

— Pope Leo XIV, Magnifica Humanitas, paragraph 9

Questions for discussion

  • Has a useful tool changed the way you relate to someone?
  • What matters in your life that cannot be measured by how much you get done?
  • Who should help decide how the technologies we use are designed?
  • Which parts of this account of human dignity make sense to you? Which would you question?
