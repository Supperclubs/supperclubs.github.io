Before we meet

  • Choose 2–3 quotes from the shared edition.
  • Note the book and passage numbers so everyone can find them.
  • Think about what drew you to each quote and what you’d like to ask the group.

When we meet

We’ll take turns reading our quotes and saying why we chose them. Then we’ll discuss them together. There’s no need to prepare a talk or have a settled view.
