Make yourself at home.

Picture a cosy living room, open books, and time to talk. Ten people who came as strangers and are slowly becoming friends.

Someone asks a question that doesn't have an easy answer. The room gets quiet for a moment—then alive. That's Supper Club.

Once a month, at someone's home. A short reading beforehand, a conversation shared together, and the kind of evening you think about for days after.
