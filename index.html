One passage.
Different ways of seeing.

We read short extracts from books and letters. Sometimes a single sentence is enough to start a conversation. This passage from Plato led us to ask how much we can trust what we see.

Read more from this gathering →
“To them, the truth would be literally nothing but the shadows of the images.”

Plato · Republic, Book VII
Translated by Benjamin Jowett

Our question

Are we prisoners of our own perception?

How we meet

About ten people, a quiet room and a little time set aside. We meet roughly once a month at someone’s home.

You don’t need to know the author or have an answer ready. Read with care, bring your questions and be willing to listen.

01

Start with a question

Something we’re curious about and want to understand better.

02

Read a short passage

We send the reading ahead of time, then return to it together.

03

Make time to talk

We share our thoughts, ask questions and give everyone time to speak.

What might we talk about?

You can bring a question, a doubt or a different view.

"Are we prisoners of our own perception?"
"What do we mean by love?"
“Which of your beliefs is hardest to explain?”
“What would a fairer society ask of you?”
