About

What is Supper Club?

Supper Club is a group of about ten people who meet at home to read and talk. We aim to meet once a month, with a short text to give each conversation a starting point.

We share ideas, ask questions and get to know one another. The group stays small so everyone has time to speak.

How it works

Before we meet, we share a short reading and a question to think about.

When we meet, we return to the passage. Each person can share what they noticed, what puzzled them or what they disagree with.

As we talk, we leave room for different views. You’re welcome to change your mind or leave a question open.

An evening together

The times are a guide. We leave room for the conversation to take its own course.

7:00 PM Arrive. Say hello and settle in.
7:30 PM Opening question. A first thought from each of us.
8:00 PM Main discussion. Read closely and follow the questions that arise.
9:30 PM Wind down. Share a last thought or a question to take home.

Who is it for?

Anyone who enjoys reading, conversation and hearing how other people see the world. You don’t need a background in philosophy. Curiosity and a willingness to listen are enough.

The readings are short. We spend our time on a few passages rather than trying to cover a whole book.

Would you like to join?

Write to us if you’d like to come along or ask a question about the group.
